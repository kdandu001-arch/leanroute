# Code of Conduct

Leanroute follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback gracefully, and keep
discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/kdandu001-arch/leanroute/security/advisories/new) or a direct
message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
